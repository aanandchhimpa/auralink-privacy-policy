Privacy Policy

AuraLink

Last updated: 9 November 2026

AuraLink ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Aanand kumar ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and why.

1. Information That Stays on Your Device

Your profile, including the name and optional gender you enter, your progress, stars, Aura balance, achievements, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive this information.

Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset all progress deletes this locally stored information from your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a backup copy is also kept in your Play Games account (see section 3).

2. Information Sent to Third-Party Services

To understand which puzzles are enjoyable, identify puzzles that may be too difficult, and fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may receive the following information:

  • Gameplay events: Which puzzle was started, finished, or left, how long it took, and how many hints were used.
  • Feature use: Whether a Share or Invite button was used (and for what kind of result), whether a reminder was turned on or off, and whether a leaderboard or the review prompt was opened.
  • Install source: If you installed AuraLink from a link a friend shared, the campaign tag on that link (for example "friend_share"). This tells us how many installs came from sharing, never who shared or who installed.
  • Usage information: When the app is opened and how long it is used.
  • Crash reports: Technical details about an error and the app's state when the error occurred.
  • Device information: Device model, Android version, app version, language, and approximate country derived from your IP address. The app does not collect your precise location.
  • Identifiers: A random app-instance ID created by Firebase and your device's advertising ID, which is used by Firebase for analytics only.

This information does not contain your name or anything you type into the app. We use it only to improve the game and do not use it to advertise to you or sell it. Google handles this information as our service provider.

Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.

The app also uses Firebase Remote Config to receive game-balance settings, such as how much Aura a board pays. To do this it sends Google a random Firebase installation ID and the app version, nothing about you or your play.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Google Play Games (Optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, AuraLink sends it your star total, the number of puzzles solved, your Daily Challenge times, and the achievements you earn, so they appear on Play Games leaderboards and your Play Games profile.

AuraLink also keeps a backup of your progress (puzzles solved, stars, Aura, profile name, items, and achievements in the game) in your Play Games account, so a reinstall or a new phone can restore it. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your Play Games account details or your backup. If you are not signed in to Play Games, nothing is sent.

4. Sharing and Invites (Optional)

When you tap Share or Invite friends, the app draws a picture of your result on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and post or send it yourself. What you share is then handled by that app (for example WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook) under its own privacy policy.

The shared link is the AuraLink Google Play page with a campaign tag. It contains no information about you.

5. Reminders (Optional)

If you turn on a reminder in Settings → Reminders, it is scheduled on your device. No information is sent to us to make it work. Turn the switch off to stop it.

6. Your Choices

  • Turn off Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage stats to stop sending gameplay events and crash reports at any time.
  • You can reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings.
  • Because AuraLink has no account system, the analytics and crash data described above cannot be linked by us to your name or account.

7. Account and Data Deletion Requests

AuraLink does not provide user accounts, and profile and game information is stored locally on your device (and, if you use Play Games, in your own Play Games backup). You can delete this local information by uninstalling the app or using Settings → Reset all progress.

If you would like to request deletion of analytics or crash data, email us with your approximate usage dates and any relevant technical details. We will review the request and do our best to help.

Data deletion contact: chhimpaaanand@gmail.com

Please include "AuraLink Data Deletion Request" in the subject line.

8. Children's Privacy

AuraLink is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at chhimpaaanand@gmail.com.

9. Ads and Purchases

This version of AuraLink shows no ads and sells nothing. If a future version adds advertising or in-app purchases, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

10. Third-Party SDK Privacy Policies

AuraLink currently uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Google Play Games Services. The following links provide additional information about commonly used Google services and SDKs:

  • Google Play Services and Play Games: Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase: Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob: AdMob and Data Protection — not used in the current version.
  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Privacy Information — not used separately from Firebase in the current version.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the app. Firebase processes analytics and crash information using Google's security infrastructure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change. We will post changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
